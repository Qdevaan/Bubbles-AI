Listening.
In plain English

Think of a calm, clever friend in your ear during any important conversation — an interview, a hard negotiation, a tense call. Bubbles listens along and quietly suggests what to say, so you never freeze.

Afterwards, just ask it questions about how it went. It remembers the people and topics you discuss, tracks your progress over time, and turns your slip-ups into practice. No jargon, no setup ritual — open it, talk, and get better at talking.

Everything you need to speak well, in one quiet app.

How it works
i.

You talk

The Flutter app captures the conversation by voice or text, on any device.

ii.

The brain listens

A fast async FastAPI backend routes each request through its /v1 API.

iii.

AI responds

An LLM router picks the best model — Gemini → Cerebras → Groq — with graceful failover and your memory in context.

iv.

Bubbles remembers

Everything is stored in Supabase; background workers extract entities and build your drills.

Built with a modern stack.

Colophon
Client
Flutter & Dart — one codebase, six platforms · Riverpod state · local SQLite cache with stale-while-revalidate · LiveKit for realtime audio · wake-word, STT & TTS.
Server
FastAPI async on Python 3.11+ · SQLAlchemy 2 & asyncpg · Alembic migrations · circuit-breaking LLM router · Redis + ARQ background jobs.
Intelligence
Gemini, Cerebras & Groq behind one router · text-embedding-004 with local ONNX fallback · Groq Whisper STT · Edge-TTS · knowledge graph via NetworkX.
Foundations
Supabase Postgres with row-level security · Docker · observability with Sentry, Prometheus & OpenTelemetry · tooling: uv, ruff, mypy, pytest.
