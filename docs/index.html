What is Bubbles, in plain English?

Think of a calm, clever friend in your ear during any important conversation — an interview, a negotiation, a tough call. Bubbles listens along and suggests what to say next, so you never freeze. Afterwards, just ask it questions about how it went. It remembers the people and topics you discuss, tracks your progress, and turns your slip-ups into practice. 🫧

How it works

A Flutter client, a fast async brain, and AI that fails over gracefully.

📱 You talk

The Flutter app captures the conversation by voice or text on any device.

⚡ Brain processes

The FastAPI backend routes the request through its /v1 API.

🧠 AI responds

An LLM router picks the best model — Gemini → Cerebras → Groq — with auto-failover, using your memory + knowledge graph.

🗄️ Bubbles remembers

Everything is stored in Supabase; background workers extract entities, score sessions, and build drills.
